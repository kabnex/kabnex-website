how we work

Five steps, every project.

01

Discover

We start with what the project actually needs to do, not the feature list — a short call to get the brief straight.

02

Design

Wireframes or visual drafts, depending on the service — enough to agree on direction before anything is built.

03

Build

Development, editing, or design production happens in short cycles, with check-ins instead of one big reveal.

04

Launch

We ship the site, app, video, or asset set — tested, and handed over with what you need to use it.

05

Support

Hosting, updates, and maintenance continue for as long as you need them — a project doesn't end at launch.
